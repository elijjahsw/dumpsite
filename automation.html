Forward/Reverse Interlocking Circuit

  • Platform: OpenPLC Editor (IEC 61131-3 Standard)
  • Language: Ladder Logic (LD)
  • Application: Bidirectional 3-Phase Motor Control

Circuit Logic & Protection

  • Software Interlocking: Included NC contacts cross-interlocking (motor_rev on forward rung;motor_fwd on reverse rung) to prevent simultaneous coil energization and phase shorts.
  • Latching (Seal-in Circuit): Implemented parallel auxiliary contact logic around momentary startF_btn and startR_btn inputs to maintain hold-in.
  • Fault & Safety Interruption: Master series placement for ol_btn and stop_btn to instantly drop out output coils under fault conditions.
